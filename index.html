Glastonbury, Connecticut

Glastonbury is a town of 35,159 people on the east bank of the Connecticut River, seven miles southeast of Hartford. First settled in 1636 and incorporated in 1693, it is a town of riverfront trails, a working Main Street, and neighborhoods that stretch from the historic South Glastonbury district to East Glastonbury and Addison. This guide collects what residents and visitors actually need: what to do, where the local businesses are, and how to reach town services.
