Things to Do in Glastonbury

Four places to start, and a town calendar that changes with the season.

Parks & Landmarks

  1. 1

    Riverfront Park

    Glastonbury's riverfront park, with trails, picnic spots and open views across the Connecticut River. The town's own list of local attractions starts here.

    Source: Town of Glastonbury — Local Attractions ↗opens in a new tab

  2. 2

    Hubbard Green

    A historic green in the center of town, used for community events and gatherings — the closest thing Glastonbury has to the traditional New England town common.

    Source: CTvisit — Town of Glastonbury ↗opens in a new tab

  3. 3

    Heritage Park

    Walking paths, playgrounds and open space — the easiest of the three to bring children to.

    Source: Tripadvisor — Things to Do in Glastonbury ↗opens in a new tab

  4. 4

    Seasonal festivals and local events

    Glastonbury holds annual events and festivals in its parks through the year. Dates and details change season to season, so check the local listings before you go.

    Source: Glastonbury local events and news ↗opens in a new tab

A note on what this page does not claim

This page lists the places the town and public listings describe as Glastonbury's main attractions. It does not list opening hours, parking rules, or facility details, because those change and can't be kept accurate here. Confirm details with the Town of Glastonbury ↗opens in a new tab before making a trip.
