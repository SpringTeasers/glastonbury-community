Town Services & Links

The official destinations for the services residents ask about most. Every link on this page is checked and goes to a real town or district site.

Town Government

  • Town of Glastonbury — main site

    Official town website: news, department directory, forms, permits, and the town's community calendar.

    • glastonburyct.gov ↗opens in a new tab

  • Town Hall — contact & hours

    2155 Main Street, Glastonbury, CT 06033. Open Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m., excluding national holidays. Main number: (860) 652-7710.

    • glastonburyct.gov/our-community/about-us/contact-us ↗opens in a new tab

  • Town department directory

    Every town department in one list — including the Town Clerk, Assessor, Building, Public Works and more.

    • glastonburyct.gov/departments ↗opens in a new tab

Health

  • Glastonbury Health Department

    The Health Department provides public health services for the town, and hosts seasonal vaccination clinics each year. No clinic dates are published on this site — the town website is the authoritative source.

    • glastonburyct.gov/departments ↗opens in a new tab

Library

  • Welles-Turner Memorial Library

    Glastonbury's public library — collections, programs and community spaces for all ages, with its own events calendar separate from the town's.

    • wtmlib.info ↗opens in a new tab
    • glastonburyct.gov — Welles-Turner Memorial Library ↗opens in a new tab

Schools

  • Glastonbury Public Schools

    The town's K–12 school district — roughly 5,600 students and about 500 certified teachers across its schools. About 95% of school-age children in Glastonbury attend public school here.

    • glastonburyus.org ↗opens in a new tab
    • glastonburyus.org/district-info ↗opens in a new tab
    • glastonburyus.org/our-schools ↗opens in a new tab

  • Adult & Continuing Education

    Run by Glastonbury Public Schools, offering classes for adults and high school students.

    • glastonburyus.org/community ↗opens in a new tab

Services the research did not verify
